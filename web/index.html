      What this can and can’t see

      wifi-sentry watches how the Wi-Fi signal between this computer and your router fluctuates; people moving through it disturb the signal. For now it’s a coarse sensor that reads the Mac’s signal strength. It picks up presence and larger movement near the path between the computer and the router, and can miss small or distant motion elsewhere in the home. Adding ESP32 sensors around your home widens what it covers.

      Phone alerts

      Sent only while armed: one per motion event, and at most one every few minutes.

      Settings

      Sensitivity

      Higher catches smaller and more distant movement but raises more false alarms. Lower ignores small things like a pet or a curtain, and can miss someone moving far from the sensor.

      Walk test

      Find out what this setup can actually see. Start the test, walk normally through the rooms you want covered for 30 to 60 seconds, then stop. You’ll get a recommended sensitivity.

      Baseline

      The detector learns what a still room looks like. Relearn after moving the computer or the router, or rearranging furniture.

      Keep the room still until learning finishes: nobody walking around, fans and robot vacuums off. Motion isn’t detected while it learns.
